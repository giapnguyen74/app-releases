DarkSurf Support

DarkSurf is a private web browser for Mac, iPhone and iPad: incognito by design, with a per-site ad filter, a session lock and an on-device assistant.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: giapnguyen74@gmail.com. Please include your device, system version and DarkSurf version (Settings → About).

Common questions

Where are my tabs after I closed the app? DarkSurf keeps nothing after it quits — every session is incognito. Add the sites you use often to Favorites.

I forgot my session PIN. The PIN is never stored. A wrong PIN opens a new, empty session; the locked one can't be recovered.

The assistant says no model is installed. Open Settings → Models and download one. It runs on your device; nothing you read or ask is sent anywhere.

A site won't open over HTTP. DarkSurf uses HTTPS only. If a site has no HTTPS, its error page lets you allow it.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: DarkSurf collects no data.
