DarkSurf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

DarkSurf is a private web browser for Mac, iPhone and iPad. In short: we don't collect any data about you. DarkSurf has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you browse, type or ask is sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Browsing: every session is incognito. Cookies, website data, history and open tabs are kept in memory and are deleted when the app quits.
  • Settings you choose: your search engine, favorites, the sites you allow over plain HTTP, the sites you turn the ad filter on for, and your on-device model choice. These stay on your device until you change them or delete the app.
  • On-device models: AI models you choose to download stay on your device until you delete them in Settings.
  • Session lock: the PIN is never stored; only a salted hash is kept in memory while the session is locked.

Network requests the app makes

  • The websites you visit, directly, like any browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • Your search engine, only when you search (DarkSurf sends nothing while you type).
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co), only when you open the Models settings (to check the signed list of available models) or download a model. These requests carry no cookies and no information about you or your browsing.
  • Updates (Mac only): the Mac app checks GitHub (github.com) for new versions.

The on-device assistant

Summaries, answers and translations are produced by a model running on your device. The text of the page or your question never leaves the device.

Apple services

DarkSurf turns off the web features that would send your browsing to Apple, such as Safe Browsing lookups, Look Up, Translate and Writing Tools inside web pages. If you have turned on iCloud Private Relay in your system settings, your device's DNS lookups go through it as for every app; DarkSurf doesn't control that setting.

Children

DarkSurf collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
